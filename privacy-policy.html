Hesapp Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hesapp ("the App") handles user information. Hesapp is developed by Gülay ADIGÜZEL.

1. Data Collection

Hesapp does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Offline Operation

Hesapp is designed to work completely offline. The App does not require an internet connection to provide its core functionality and does not send user-entered information to a remote server.

3. Device Permissions

Hesapp does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or other sensitive device data.

4. Third-Party Services

Hesapp does not use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, or other services that collect or transmit users' personal information.

5. Data Storage

Hesapp does not maintain a remote database containing users' personal information. Any information created or entered within the App, if stored locally for the App's functionality, remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties by Hesapp.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Hesapp does not collect or maintain users' personal information on external servers, the developer does not retain users' personal data.

Users can remove locally stored App data by using the device's app settings or by uninstalling Hesapp, subject to the operating system's behavior.

7. Children's Privacy

Hesapp does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
